Who we are

Partniva is a specialist partner-discovery platform built for the European data centre delivery market.

What Partniva does

We help project teams identify relevant contractors, suppliers and specialist delivery partners by country and capability. Each company included in the public directory is supported by an audited country-and-category claim, so users can search for the exact type of partner they need in the market where they need them.

Keeping the matrix current

Partniva is not intended to be a static directory. We routinely review the market for additional companies that should be included, re-check existing entries, and remove or amend companies where the available evidence no longer supports their listing. This ongoing review is designed to keep the contractor matrix as current, useful and evidence-led as possible.

Our approach

Verification is based on publicly available company and project evidence, with country coverage and capability reviewed separately. Inclusion does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee of performance, or commercial recommendation; it means the company has met Partniva's evidence threshold for the specific verified claims shown in the directory.
