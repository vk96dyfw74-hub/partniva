Who we are

Partniva is a specialist partner discovery platform built for the global data centre market.

What Partniva does

We help project teams identify relevant contractors, suppliers and specialist delivery partners by region, country and capability. Each company included in the Partniva directory is supported by verified evidence for the markets and capabilities in which they operate, helping users identify the right partners for their projects.

Keeping the matrix current

Partniva is not a static directory. We routinely review data centre markets for additional companies, re-check existing entries, and amend or remove companies where the available evidence no longer supports their listing. As Partniva expands into new regions, the matrix will continue to grow while maintaining the same evidence-led verification approach.

Our approach

Verification is based on publicly available company and project evidence, with country coverage and capability reviewed separately. Inclusion does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee of performance, or commercial recommendation; it means the company has met Partniva's evidence threshold for the specific verified claims shown in the directory.
