Privacy notice

This notice explains the main personal data Partniva processes when you create an account and use the platform.

Who is responsible

The service is operated by Partniva. Contact: hello@partniva.com.

Last updated: 28 August 2026.

Information we process

  • Account information such as name, work email, company and job title.
  • Organisation membership, subscription status and billing-related identifiers.
  • Your private contractor ratings and private notes.
  • Organisation-shared Shortlist and Engagement records.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate authentication and the service.

Why we process it

We process account and workspace information to provide Partniva, secure access, maintain subscriptions, support users, protect the service and keep organisation workspaces functioning. Where billing is connected, payment-card details should be collected by the payment provider rather than stored by Partniva.

Private supplier information

Personal star ratings and private supplier notes are intended to be visible only to the user who created them. Organisation Shortlists and Engagements may be visible to other authorised users within the same organisation.

Retention, rights and requests

Data is retained while required to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or protect legitimate business interests. Requests concerning access, correction, deletion or other applicable data-protection rights can be sent to hello@partniva.com.
