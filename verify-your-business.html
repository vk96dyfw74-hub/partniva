BUSINESS VERIFICATION

Get your business verified

If your business delivers data centre work or relevant specialist supply-chain services, you can ask Partniva to review it for inclusion.

What to send us

  • Company legal or trading name and working website.
  • Countries where you actively deliver work or supply services.
  • The exact disciplines and capabilities you want reviewed.
  • Service pages, project references, case studies or other delivery evidence.
  • A contact name and business email address.

How verification works

Capability and geography are assessed separately. A claim is only published where the available evidence supports the country, discipline and exact scope. Partniva may request further evidence or decline a claim.

Request a review

Email verify@partniva.com. Submission does not guarantee inclusion.
