Get your business verified

If your business works in data centre delivery or a relevant specialist supply chain, you can ask Partniva to review it for inclusion.

What to send us

  • Company legal/trading name and website.
  • Countries where you actively deliver work or supply services.
  • The partner categories you believe are relevant.
  • Links to service pages, project references, case studies or other evidence.
  • A contact name and business email address.

How verification works

We review capability and geography separately. A company is only listed for country-and-category combinations where the available evidence supports the claim. We may ask for additional information or decline a category if the evidence is not sufficiently clear.

Request a review

Email verify@partniva.com with the information above. Submitting a request does not guarantee inclusion.
