Cookie & storage settings

Partniva currently uses essential browser storage required for sign-in and workspace functionality.

Essential storage

Partniva and its authentication provider may use essential cookies or browser storage to keep users signed in securely. The application also uses local browser storage as a resilience/cache layer for selected workspace state.

Optional analytics

Partniva should not enable non-essential analytics or advertising cookies until an appropriate consent mechanism is in place. If optional technologies are introduced, this page and the consent controls should be updated before they are enabled.

Browser controls

You can remove site data through your browser settings. Removing essential authentication storage may sign you out, and locally cached workspace data may need to be downloaded again from Partniva after you sign back in.
