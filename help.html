Help & support

Guidance on search, verification, private supplier memory and Engagements.

Finding a partner

Select a region and country, choose a discipline if useful, then describe the exact capability you need. Exact verified subtype matches are prioritised above broader evidence-text matches.

What “Partniva Verified” means

A published verified result means Partniva has evidence supporting the specific capability and geographic claim shown. It does not guarantee current capacity, price, financial standing, performance or suitability for a specific project.

Shortlist, ratings and Engagements

Your star rating and private supplier note are personal to your user account. On Team and Business plans, Shortlist and Engagements are shared at organisation level so colleagues can see the active supplier relationship without exposing each person's private opinion.

Why a company may change or disappear

Claims can be amended, suspended or removed when evidence changes, a market can no longer be supported, or a verification review no longer passes Partniva's requirements.

Need more help?

Email support@partniva.com and include the country, search term and company name where relevant.
