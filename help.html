Help & support

Guidance on using Partniva and understanding the verified partner results.

Finding a partner

Select a region and country, enter what you are looking for or choose a partner type, then press Search. Partniva only returns companies with a verified claim for the selected country and capability.

What “Verified” means

A verified result means Partniva has evidence supporting that company's listed capability and geographic coverage. It does not mean Partniva has audited commercial performance, financial standing, pricing, capacity or suitability for a specific project.

Why a company may disappear

Listings may change as our market review continues. A company can be added, updated or removed if new evidence becomes available, if its service coverage changes, or if a previous claim can no longer be supported.

Need more help?

Email support@partniva.com and include the country, search term and company name where relevant.
